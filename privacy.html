In English

1. Information we collect

Only what you voluntarily provide: your name, phone number, district/area/village, your work skills, and — if you allow it — your device location (latitude/longitude).

2. Why we collect it

Solely to show you nearby work and to show nearby workers to employers. Location is used to calculate distance.

3. What is publicly visible

The details you register (name, phone number, location area, skills) are shown publicly to other WorkPin users so they can contact you directly. By providing your phone number you consent to it being visible to others.

4. What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third-party advertisers. We never ask for bank or payment details.

5. Location

Sharing location is optional. WorkPin works if you deny it — distances are then shown approximately.

6. Where data is stored

Securely in Google Firebase (Firestore).
